# Company Policies — Northwind Outfitters (SAMPLE DATA)

Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company used only as sample data for this demo.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Core collaboration hours
are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive **20 days of paid time off per year**, plus 10 paid
public holidays. PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
for absences longer than 3 days.

## Expenses

Employees can expense up to **$75 per month** for home-office supplies without
prior approval. Larger purchases need manager approval.
