# AeroDesk Pro — Product FAQ (SAMPLE DATA)

The AeroDesk Pro is a fictional product used only as sample data for this demo.

## What is the weight limit?

The AeroDesk Pro supports up to **150 kg** (330 lbs) of evenly distributed weight.

## What is the height range?

The desk adjusts electrically from **62 cm to 128 cm** (24 to 50 inches).

## Warranty

The AeroDesk Pro comes with a **5-year warranty** covering the motor, frame,
and electronics. The warranty does not cover cosmetic damage or misuse.

## Assembly

Assembly takes about **45 minutes** with two people. All tools are included in the box.
