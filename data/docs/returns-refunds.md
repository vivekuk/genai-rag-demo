# Returns and Refunds Policy — Northwind Outfitters (SAMPLE DATA)

Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company used only as sample data for this demo.

## Returns

Customers can return most items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the order number.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days**
after the returned item arrives at our warehouse.

## Exceptions

Final-sale items, gift cards, and opened software cannot be returned.
Damaged or defective items can be returned at any time within 90 days, and
Northwind Outfitters will cover the return shipping cost for defective items.
