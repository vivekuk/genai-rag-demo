# Shipping Information — Northwind Outfitters (SAMPLE DATA)

Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company used only as sample data for this demo.

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes **3 to 5 business days**. Express shipping takes
**1 to 2 business days** and is available at checkout.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is **free for orders over $50**. Orders under $50 cost $6.95.
Express shipping costs a flat **$14.95**.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom. International delivery takes
**7 to 14 business days** and customs fees are the customer's responsibility.
